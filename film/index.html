RipWatch film scenes

Standalone 1920×1080 scenes for the judging video, scaled to fit the window. Open one full screen and start the screen recorder. Space or → plays the next beat, R restarts the scene. Pressing a key while a beat is still playing finishes that beat first. Needs internet access once per page load, for GSAP from cdnjs.
